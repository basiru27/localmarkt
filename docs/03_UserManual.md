# GMarkt User Manual

## The Gambia's Classifieds Marketplace

---

### 3.1 Getting Started

#### 3.1.1 Accessing GMarkt

Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari) and navigate to **https://gmarkt.tech**. The site loads as a normal webpage, but you can also install it as an app on your phone or computer — look for an "Install" or "Add to Home Screen" option in your browser's menu.

#### 3.1.2 Creating an Account

1. Tap the **"Sign Up"** or **"Register"** button in the top navigation bar.
2. Enter your **email address** and choose a **password** (at least 6 characters).
3. Enter your **display name** — this is what other users will see.
4. Enter your **phone number** in Gambian format (e.g., `+220 7654321`).
5. Tap **"Create Account"**.
6. Check your email inbox for a confirmation message from GMarkt and click the confirmation link.
7. Once confirmed, return to GMarkt and sign in.

#### 3.1.3 Logging In and Out

- **To log in**: Tap **"Login"** in the navigation bar, enter your email and password, then tap **"Sign In"**.
- **To stay logged in across sessions**: Check the "Remember me" option during login.
- **To log out**: Tap your profile icon or menu button in the navigation bar, then tap **"Logout"**.
- **If you forget your password**: Tap **"Forgot Password?"** on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

#### 3.1.4 Setting Up Your Profile

1. Log in and tap on **"Profile"** from the navigation menu.
2. You can update:
   - **Display name**: Your public name shown on listings and reviews
   - **Phone number**: Buyers will use this to contact you
   - **Bio**: A short description about yourself or what you sell
   - **Avatar**: Tap the profile picture area to upload a photo of yourself
3. Tap **"Save"** to apply changes.

### 3.2 For Buyers

#### 3.2.1 Browsing Listings

The home page shows a feed of all available listings, starting with the most recently posted. Scrolling down loads more listings automatically. Each listing card shows the item photo, title, price in Gambian Dalasi (GMD), location, and time since posting.

#### 3.2.2 Using Search

1. Tap the search bar at the top of the page.
2. Type what you are looking for (e.g., "iPhone", "sofa", "generator").
3. As you type, suggestions will appear — tap one to search instantly.
4. Press Enter or tap the search icon to see all matching results.
5. To clear your search, tap the "X" icon in the search bar.
6. Your recent searches appear as chips below the search bar. Tap a chip to repeat that search. Tap the "X" on a chip to remove it, or tap "Clear" to remove all search history.

#### 3.2.3 Filtering by Category

1. Browse the home page or use the category dropdown/filter bar.
2. Select a category (e.g., "Electronics & Phones", "Clothing & Apparel", "Vehicles").
3. The feed updates to show only listings in that category.
4. To remove the filter, select "All Categories" or clear the selection.

#### 3.2.4 Filtering by Location (Zone and Area)

1. Open the location filter (tap the location icon or dropdown).
2. First, select a **zone** (e.g., "Serrekunda", "Bakau / Fajara", "Kololi / Kotu").
3. Then select a specific **area** within that zone (e.g., "Kanifing", "Bakau", "Kololi").
4. The feed updates to show only listings in the selected area.
5. To remove the filter, clear your area selection.

#### 3.2.5 Sorting Results

Use the sort dropdown to change the order of listings:
- **Latest** (default): Newest and bumped listings first
- **Price: Low to High**: Cheapest first
- **Price: High to Low**: Most expensive first
- **Most Viewed**: Most popular listings first
- **Oldest First**: Listings that have been up the longest

#### 3.2.6 Reading a Listing Detail Page

Tap any listing card to open its full details. The detail page shows:
- Large images of the item (swipe or tap arrows to browse)
- The seller's display name and profile picture
- Price and whether it is negotiable
- Item condition (New, Like New, Good, or Fair)
- Location (zone and area)
- Full description
- Average rating and review count
- An expiry indicator if the listing is over 60 days old

#### 3.2.7 Contacting a Seller via WhatsApp

1. Open any approved listing's detail page.
2. Tap the **"WhatsApp"** button (green icon).
3. Your phone's WhatsApp app will open with a pre-filled message: *"Hi! I'm interested in your listing on GMarkt."*
4. Send the message to start chatting with the seller.
5. You must be logged in to see the WhatsApp button — contact details are hidden from guests.

#### 3.2.8 Calling a Seller

1. Open any approved listing's detail page.
2. Tap the **"Call"** button (phone icon).
3. Your phone's dialler will open with the seller's number pre-filled.
4. Tap the call button to connect.

#### 3.2.9 Saving a Listing

When you find a listing you are interested in, tap the **heart icon** on the listing card or detail page to save it. The heart will fill in to show it is saved. Tap the heart again to unsave. You must be logged in to save listings.

#### 3.2.10 Viewing Saved Listings

1. Log in and tap **"Saved"** in the navigation menu.
2. You will see all your saved listings in one place.
3. Tap any listing to open its full details.
4. Tap the heart icon on the saved page to remove a listing from your saved collection.

#### 3.2.11 Sharing a Listing

1. Open the listing you want to share.
2. Tap the **"Share"** button.
3. If you are on a phone, the native share menu will appear — select WhatsApp, Telegram, or any app to share the link.
4. If native sharing is not available (desktop browsers), the link is automatically copied to your clipboard. A confirmation message will appear.

#### 3.2.12 Leaving a Review

1. You can leave a review **only after you have contacted the seller** via the WhatsApp or Call button on the listing.
2. Open the listing detail page. If you are eligible to review, a **"Write a Review"** section will appear.
3. Tap a star rating (1–5 stars). 5 stars is the best.
4. Optionally, write a short comment about your experience.
5. Tap **"Submit Review"**.
6. You can edit or delete your review later by returning to the listing.

#### 3.2.13 Reporting a Listing

If you see a listing that violates GMarkt's guidelines (e.g., fake item, wrong category, inappropriate content):

1. Open the listing detail page.
2. Tap the **"Report"** button (usually a flag icon).
3. Select or type a reason for your report.
4. Optionally add more details.
5. Tap **"Submit Report"**. An admin will review it.

### 3.3 For Sellers

#### 3.3.1 Posting a New Listing

1. Log in and tap **"Sell"** or **"Create Listing"** in the navigation bar.
2. Fill in the form step by step:
   - **Title**: A clear, descriptive title (e.g., "Samsung Galaxy A24 — Good Condition")
   - **Description**: Details about the item — condition, age, reason for selling, features
   - **Category**: Select the most appropriate category
   - **Price**: Enter your price in Gambian Dalasi
   - **Condition**: Select New, Like New, Good, or Fair
   - **Zone**: Select the area where the item is located
   - **Area**: Select the specific neighbourhood
   - **Images**: Tap the image upload area to take a photo or select from your gallery. You can add up to 5 images. Images are automatically compressed to save data.
   - **Phone Number**: Your contact number for buyers
3. Tap **"Post Listing"**.
4. Your listing will be reviewed by an admin and will appear on the feed once approved.

**If you are offline**: You can still fill out the form while offline. The listing will be saved on your phone and automatically posted when you reconnect to the internet. A badge will show how many items are waiting to be synced.

#### 3.3.2 Editing a Listing

1. Go to **"My Listings"** from the navigation menu.
2. Find the listing you want to edit and tap the **"Edit"** button.
3. Make your changes to any field.
4. Tap **"Save Changes"**.
5. Note: edited listings are set back to "pending" status and must be re-approved by an admin.

#### 3.3.3 Deleting a Listing

1. Go to **"My Listings"**.
2. Find the listing and tap the **"Delete"** button.
3. Confirm that you want to delete it. The listing and its images will be permanently removed.

#### 3.3.4 Marking a Listing as Sold

1. Go to **"My Listings"**.
2. Find the listing and tap the **"Mark as Sold"** button.
3. The listing will be marked with a "Sold" badge and will no longer appear in search results.
4. To undo this, tap the same button again (it will now say "Mark as Available").

#### 3.3.5 Relisting a Sold Listing

1. Go to your sold listing in **"My Listings"**.
2. Tap **"Mark as Available"** to relist it. The listing will move back to pending status for admin approval.

#### 3.3.6 Bumping a Listing to the Top

Bumping moves your listing to the top of the feed so more people see it.

1. Go to **"My Listings"**.
2. Find an approved, active listing and tap the **"Bump to Top"** button.
3. You can bump only **one listing every 24 hours**. If you recently bumped another listing, you will be told which listing was bumped and how long to wait.

#### 3.3.7 Understanding the Analytics Dashboard

The Analytics Dashboard shows how your listings are performing. Access it from **"My Listings" → "Analytics"**.

**Date Range Selection**: Tap the date filter button to view data from the last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or all time.

**Stat Cards** at the top show:
- **Total Views**: How many times your listings were viewed
- **Contacts**: How many buyers clicked WhatsApp or Call
- **Saves**: How many buyers saved your listings
- **Active**: How many of your listings are currently live and for sale
- **Sold**: How many listings you have marked as sold

**Views Chart**: A line chart showing how your listing views trended over the selected time period.

**Listing Performance Table**: A sortable table showing each of your listings with its view count, contact count, and save count. Use this to see which listings are getting the most attention and which might need better photos or descriptions.

### 3.4 For Administrators

#### 3.4.1 Accessing the Admin Console

If you have been granted the admin role, you will see an **"Admin"** link in your navigation menu. Tap it to open the Admin Console.

#### 3.4.2 Dashboard Overview

When you first enter the Admin Console (tap **"Dashboard"**), you will see:
- **Stat cards**: Total users, banned users, total listings, pending listings, pending reports
- **New Listings Chart**: A bar chart showing how many listings were created each day over the last 14 days
- **Recent Actions**: A list of the most recent admin actions on the platform

#### 3.4.3 Moderating Listings

1. Tap **"Listings"** in the Admin Console sidebar.
2. Use the **status filter** to show "pending" listings (those awaiting approval).
3. Review each listing's title, description, images, and price.
4. To **approve**: Tap the green checkmark button. The listing will go live on the feed.
5. To **reject**: Tap the red "X" button. Optionally enter a reason explaining why it was rejected (the seller will see this).
6. Use the search bar to find specific listings by title.

#### 3.4.4 Managing Users

1. Tap **"Users"** in the Admin Console sidebar.
2. Browse the user list or search by display name.
3. To **ban a user**: Find the user and tap the **"Ban"** button. The user will be immediately suspended and cannot log in. Enter a reason for the ban (optional).
4. To **unban a user**: Find a banned user and tap the **"Unban"** button. Their account will be restored.
5. To **verify a seller**: Find a trustworthy seller and tap the **"Verify"** button. A verified badge will appear on their profile.
6. To **permanently delete a user** (super admin only): Use the **"Delete"** button. This permanently removes the user and all their data.

**Note**: You cannot ban your own account. Only super admins can ban or delete other super admins.

#### 3.4.5 Handling Reports

1. Tap **"Reports"** in the Admin Console sidebar.
2. View all pending reports, showing who reported what and why.
3. To **resolve** a report: Review the reported listing or user, take appropriate action (e.g., reject the listing or ban the user), then tap **"Resolve"**.
4. To **dismiss** a report: If the report is unfounded, tap **"Dismiss"**.
5. Use the status filter to track resolved, dismissed, or re-opened reports.

#### 3.4.6 Reading the Audit Log

1. Tap **"Audit Log"** in the Admin Console sidebar (super admin only).
2. View a chronological list of every admin action performed on the platform.
3. Use filters to narrow by:
   - **Admin**: See actions by a specific admin
   - **Action**: Filter by action type (e.g., approve listing, ban user)
   - **Date range**: See actions within a specific time period
4. Each entry shows who performed the action, what they did, when, and any additional details.
